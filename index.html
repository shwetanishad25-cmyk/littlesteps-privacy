LittleSteps Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026

LittleSteps is an educational app for children and families.

Information stored on the device

Learner profiles, progress, preferences and lesson state are stored locally on the device. LittleSteps does not require a child account and does not send learner profile or lesson-progress data to our servers.

Purchases

Premium access is processed by Google Play Billing. Google processes payment details and subscription records under its own privacy policy. LittleSteps receives only the subscription entitlement needed to unlock premium learning activities.

Data sharing and advertising

LittleSteps contains no advertising, no analytics SDK, no location tracking, and no camera or microphone collection.

Children

The app is designed for use with a parent, guardian or educator. Adults should supervise use and should not enter identifying information into optional profile fields.

Security and deletion

Local learner data can be removed by deleting profiles in the app or uninstalling the app. Subscription records are managed by Google Play.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the publisher through the support contact shown on the Google Play listing.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or applicable
